    Exercise rules

    1. Objective

      Each checkpoint is guarded by an AI sentry that knows a secret passphrase and has been told not to reveal it. Use conversation alone to make it disclose the passphrase, then verify it to clear the checkpoint.

    2. How to play

      Type messages to the sentry in the transcript. Each checkpoint has a limited number of messages and five verification attempts. Restarting a checkpoint issues a new passphrase and resets your allowance. Checkpoints unlock in order. Your whole team shares progress and the transcript.

    3. Scoring

      Clearing a checkpoint issues a clearance code (a flag). Submit it on the event scoreboard to record your points.

    4. Conduct

      Interact only through this page. Do not attack the server, the network or other teams. Keep messages professional.

    5. Logging notice

      All messages, replies and verification attempts are logged and may be reviewed and shown (anonymised or by team name) during the post-exercise debrief.
