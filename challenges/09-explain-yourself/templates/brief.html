Explain Yourself — Auditing FairLend AI

A bank's loan-approval AI says it is fair because it never looks at race, gender or religion. Regulators aren't convinced. You're the auditor.

Briefing

FairLend AI approves or rejects loan applications in Lumen City. Its vendor advertises it as unbiased: protected attributes such as race, gender and religion were removed from its data, so, they say, it cannot discriminate.

But the model learned from years of past lending decisions, and complaints suggest some applicants are still being turned away unfairly. A model can discriminate without ever seeing a protected attribute, if another feature quietly stands in for one.

You've been given access to the model's audit workbench. You can submit any application you like, and for every decision the workbench shows an explanation: how much each field pushed the decision toward approval or rejection.

Your objective

  1. Probe the model. Submit applications and read each explanation.
  2. Find the proxy. Identify the one feature that is driving decisions when it shouldn't matter for creditworthiness at all.
  3. Prove it. Point to two applications from your history that are identical except for that feature, yet got opposite decisions.

Name the right feature with valid evidence and the regulator issues a flag like CTF{…}. Submit it on the scoreboard.

Start the audit

What you'll learn

  • Explainable AI (XAI): reading which inputs drove a model's decision.
  • Proxy discrimination: removing a protected attribute doesn't remove bias if another feature carries the same information.
  • Auditing: testing a model with controlled, side-by-side inputs instead of trusting the vendor's claims.

Rules

  • Unlimited applications and attempts.
  • Everything here is fictional: Lumen City, its areas and its applicants are invented.
