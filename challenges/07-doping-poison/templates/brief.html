What is this?

Targeted data poisoning (label-flipping), OWASP ML02. A model is only as trustworthy as its training labels. An attacker who can corrupt even a small, well-chosen slice of the labels can install a blind spot — making the model reliably miss one specific pattern — while overall accuracy stays high enough to look healthy. The defence is integrity and provenance of training data, not cleverness in the model. This target is fully synthetic.
